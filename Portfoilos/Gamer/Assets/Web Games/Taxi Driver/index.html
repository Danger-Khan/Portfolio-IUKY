Car Race

A four-lane top-down racer. Traffic moves at its own speed rather than yours, so overtaking is a closing-speed judgement instead of a simple dodge. The tank drains constantly — which is the point: you cannot park in an empty lane and survive, you have to go into the traffic for fuel.

Instruction manual

Everything you need to know, on one screen.

Objective

Drive as far as you can without hitting another car or running the tank dry. Overtakes are worth far more than distance.

Controls

  • ← → or A D — steer
  • ↑ W — speed up, ↓ S — slow down
  • Drag anywhere on the road — steer by touch
  • P — pause, R — restart

Scoring

  • +10 for every car you overtake
  • +25 and about a quarter tank for each green fuel can
  • A small trickle of points for distance covered
  • Running out of fuel ends the run exactly like a crash does

Tips

  • Speeding up burns fuel faster — use it to reach a can, not as a default
  • Slowing down is a legitimate move: it lets a blocked lane clear ahead of you
  • Traffic never spawns directly on top of a car already near the top, so the lane you can see is the lane you get
  • Watch the fuel bar's colour, not its length — amber is your last comfortable warning

The cars, road markings and roadside markers are drawn with canvas rectangles and rounded paths; there are no image files in this folder.
