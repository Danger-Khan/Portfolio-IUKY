Space IO

Momentum is the whole game. Thrust adds to your velocity and nothing takes it away except a light drag, so stopping means turning around and burning. Asteroids split into smaller, faster pieces when hit, which is why a nearly-clear screen is often the most dangerous one.

Instruction manual

Everything you need to know, on one screen.

Objective

Break every asteroid without being hit. Clearing the screen starts the next, larger wave. Three lives.

Controls

  • ← → or A D — rotate
  • ↑ or W — thrust
  • Space — fire
  • ↓ or S — emergency brake
  • Touch: drag to steer and thrust, tap to fire

Scoring

  • +20 for a large asteroid
  • +50 for a medium one
  • +100 for a small one — the hardest to hit
  • Everything wraps at the screen edge, including your shots

Tips

  • Shoot large rocks only when you have room — each one becomes two faster problems
  • The brake is worth learning; fighting your own momentum is what kills most runs
  • Bullets inherit your velocity, so firing while drifting sideways curves your aim
  • You are briefly invulnerable after respawning and you blink while it lasts — use it to clear space

The asteroid silhouettes are generated once per rock and then rotated, so each one keeps a consistent shape while spinning. No textures involved.
