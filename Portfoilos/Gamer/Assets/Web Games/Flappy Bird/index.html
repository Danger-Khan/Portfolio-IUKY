Flappy Bird

The classic, rebuilt properly. The bird is a real projectile under constant downward acceleration, and a flap sets its vertical velocity rather than adding to it — which is why the controls stay responsive even during a fast fall. The gap narrows every 10 points, down to a floor it never drops below.

Instruction manual

Everything you need to know, on one screen.

Objective

Fly between the pipes without touching one, the ground, or letting a gap close on you. There is no end — the score is how many pipes you cleared.

Controls

  • Space, W or ↑ — flap
  • Click or tap anywhere on the canvas — flap
  • P — pause, R — restart
  • The ceiling stops you but does not kill you

Scoring

  • +1 for every pipe you pass
  • No bonuses and no multipliers — the number is the pipe count
  • Your best is saved in this browser only

Tips

  • Tap in a steady rhythm rather than reacting; the bird responds to a flap the same way every time
  • Aim for the middle of the gap, not the edge you are closest to
  • The gap shrinks at 10, 20, 30 points — expect it and fly tighter early
  • Falling is faster than rising, so recover sooner than feels necessary

Nothing is downloaded: the bird, pipes, clouds and ground are all canvas paths, and the flap sound is a synthesised square wave.
