Capstone Project

KP Marble Slurry Contamination Risk Portal

A Google Earth Engine application that screens seven verified marble-processing clusters across six districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Peshawar, Shabqadar, Risalpur, Jehangira, Mohmand, and Buner) for slurry contamination. It combines spectral indices — NDVI, NDWI, NDBI, and a custom Slurry Index — with a Random Forest classifier and a 2018–2026 per-pixel trend model to flag sites with a rising accumulation trajectory near streams, then quantifies candidate contamination area within 100 m and 500 m buffers of each site.

Sentinel-2 Random Forest Google Earth Engine Time-Series Trend

Built as a first-pass screening tool that flags candidate sites for field verification — not a standalone determination that contamination exists.
